# Dependencies and workload provenance

The simulator depends on NumPy, distributed under its own BSD license. Python and the optional LaTeX toolchain retain their respective licenses.

The operator metadata was captured from TurboVLA for a LIBERO task. TurboVLA and LIBERO are credited in the paper bibliography. The bundled JSON contains shapes, operator arguments, schedule descriptors, and embedding index lists; it does not redistribute model checkpoints, training data, or LIBERO images.

The IEEEtran document class and LaTeX packages are supplied by the user's TeX distribution and are not vendored here. Paper figures and manuscript sources are included as the authors' research materials. The MIT license applies to the software in this repository; it does not replace the licenses of external dependencies or cited works.
